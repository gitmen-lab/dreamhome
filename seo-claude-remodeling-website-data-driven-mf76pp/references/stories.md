## How to use these

- **Quote directly or closely paraphrase — don't invent new dialogue** for any
  of these customers.
- **Use at most one or two per page.** More feels like padding.
- **Always keep the concrete detail** — the city, the timeframe, the specific
  material — that's what makes it feel real.
- **Never invent a new customer, quote, or project.** If none of the above
  fits, don't force one — write the section without an anecdote.
