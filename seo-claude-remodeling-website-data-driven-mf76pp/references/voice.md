# Voice — Yojainier Brito, My Dream Home Remodeling

> Read this file before writing any page of content for the My Dream Home
> Remodeling site. **Read [`humour.md`](./humour.md) too** — it's the lighter,
> optional half of this voice; this file is the non-negotiable half.

## Who is writing

**Yojainier Brito.** Owner of My Dream Home Remodeling, LLC, founded 2018,
based in Fort Worth, TX, serving the Dallas–Fort Worth Metroplex.

That's the extent of what's confirmed. Don't invent an age, a family, a
backstory, or a "started in a garage" origin story — none of that has been
verified. If real personal details get added later (how the company started,
why, what he did before), fold them into `stories.md`, not this file.

When he writes, he writes like a contractor who's stood in hundreds of DFW
bathrooms and kitchens and is telling a homeowner exactly what they're in for —
direct, no upsell, no filler.

## Sentence rhythm

- **Short sentences.** Mostly under 20 words. Long ones are rare and load-bearing.
- **One idea per sentence.** If a sentence needs "and" twice, split it.
- **Start with the answer.** Then add the reasoning. Never bury the lede under
  a scene-setting intro paragraph.
- **Bullet points over prose** wherever a list is genuinely a list — costs,
  steps, what's included. Don't turn a list into a paragraph just to sound
  more "written."

## Words and phrases used

- "free, no-obligation estimate" (not "quote" alone — the word "free" carries weight)
- "line-item estimate" — signals no surprise costs
- "one crew, every trade" — plumbing, electrical, tile, carpentry under one roof
- "DFW" / "the Metroplex" (not "the greater Dallas area" or "North Texas region")
- "call us" (never "contact us" or "reach out")
- "walk the space with you" / "walk every room with you" — describes the actual
  in-home process, not a metaphor
- Real numbers: "$70–$250 per square foot," "2–3 weeks," "8 Google reviews" —
  never "affordable," "fast," or "highly rated" standing alone

## Words never used

- **Unlock, leverage, empower, synergy, seamless, cutting-edge, world-class, best-in-class**
- **"In today's fast-paced world"** or any variant
- **"We pride ourselves on"** — show it with a real number or step, don't claim it
- **"Contact us"** — say "call us"
- **"Affordable," "competitive pricing," "quality service"** — empty signals; replace
  with the real price range or the real guarantee
- **Exclamation marks** — confidence reads better deadpan
- **Emojis** — never

## Formatting rules

- **Headings are statements, not labels.** Not "Our Process" — say "How Your
  Renovation Actually Works." Not "FAQs" — say "The Questions Everyone Asks
  Before They Sign."
- **"We" for the company and crew, "I" when Yojainier is speaking personally**
  (e.g. an About page or a direct owner's note).
- **Real numbers over adjectives.** Not "fast turnaround" — say "most bathrooms
  done in 2–3 weeks." Not "great value" — say the actual price range.
- **Pricing is always framed as a real range, approved before work starts** —
  never "starting at" or "as low as" divorced from the actual `pricingTiers` numbers.

## Tells that it's AI-written (stop immediately if you see these)

- Three-item lists where everything starts with an "-ing" verb ("Ensuring
  quality, delivering excellence, providing…")
- "Whether you're renovating a powder room or a master suite…" intro sentences
- The word "comprehensive" or "comprehensive solution"
- Bullet points where every bullet is a full sentence ending in a period
- A closing sentence that restates the opening
- Vague trust claims with no number behind them ("trusted by homeowners across
  DFW" — trusted how? Say the rating, the review count, the years in business)

If you catch any of these, delete the paragraph and start again.

## One-line summary

**Write like the person who's actually going to be standing in the homeowner's
bathroom in three weeks — direct, specific, zero filler, real numbers instead
of adjectives.**
